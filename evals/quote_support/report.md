# Evidence quotes: do they support the labels?

Generated by `evals/quote_support.py` (prompt `prompts/quote_support_v1.md`). No model call is billed: the judge is Claude Haiku through the Claude Pro plan (`claude -p`), reported as claude-haiku-4-5-20251001; API-equivalent $0.6588 over 11 requests (11 attempts).

**Method.** Code checks that every quote is an exact substring of its review and, on the human-labelled golden set, whether the model quoted the sentence the human chose as evidence. A blind judge then reads each review, the quote and the three labels and says, per label, whether the quote on its own supports it (`supported`), is consistent but needs the rest of the review (`partial`), or does not show it (`not_supported`). The judge sees only the final run's own labels and quotes, under neutral IDs; Eason's golden labels are read only by code, after judging, for the comparisons below. The final-run sample is a declared random sample of distinct texts (lowest SHA-256('quote-support-v1:' + review_id), excluding the labelled sets).

**Golden labels kept out of the judge.** A first version of this check (2026-10-15) also sent the 50 golden labels and evidence quotes to the judge as calibration items. Because the Doc says never to supply the human answer labels as model inputs, that version was withdrawn on 2026-10-17 and every item was judged again in batches that contain only the final run's labels. Both versions ran after the final run's labels were fixed; neither changed a label, the ranking or the memo.
